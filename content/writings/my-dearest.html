WRITING · LETTER

My Dearest

By Vanlalhmangaihzula

My dearest,

I have written this letter without a request, without the hope of changing your heart. I write only because my own heart deserves the honesty I have denied for so long.

I could never love another like I loved you. I emptied my cup of love on you, and now I have none left to give. Every drop was poured into your name, every ounce of devotion spent learning the shape of your heart.

I loved you recklessly, without reserve, without a plan for survival. I was not foolish enough to believe that such a love was possible, yet in you, I found everything I had ever wanted.

I see the details of you — even the smallest ones — and I cherish them. I love you without knowing how, or when, or from where. I love you simply, without complexity or pride. I love you so deeply that when you close your eyes, my heart finds rest beside you.

A few days ago, I thought I loved you, but after seeing you again, I realized that my love had grown a thousand times stronger. From the moment I first knew you, I have admired you more with every passing day.

Sometimes, I wish I could see your imperfections — the parts of yourself you believe are not beautiful, the moments when you are not graceful, the flaws you try to hide — because I do not want to love only the perfection the world sees. I want to love every part of who you are.

Every time I look into your eyes, there is a quiet sparkle that draws me back to you. It awakens emotions too vast and delicate to be captured by words. It shines brightly enough to bring warmth into even the darkest corners of the world.

And somewhere along the way, I realized that I had fallen in love with that light.

It was never only your eyes or your smile. It was everything about you — the way you made me feel understood, the way your presence felt like home in a world full of passing strangers.

I do not want to be only a chapter in your story. I want to be your last love. Everyone can be there in the beginning; let me at least be there for the conclusion.

The beginning belongs to affection, but the ending belongs to devotion. Anyone can love your light. Anyone can stay when the days are easy. Let me be the one who remains through the storms, the shadows, and the difficult days that life may bring.

I do not want a love that exists only in beautiful moments. I want a love that feels less like a spark and more like coming home.

For I believe souls never meet by accident. Our meeting was written before it happened, and among all the paths I could have taken, I am grateful that life led me to yours.

With all my love,

Vanlalhmangaihzula

— END —
