WRITING · SONG

Tawnmang Pâr

By NT Malsawma

“TAWNMANG PÂr”

1. Vangkhaw mawi tu I sakhmel,
An hril I sakhmel mawi;
Ami zaleng te’n an sawi,
Thachungchuangi ngei chu.

Kei tan a tawnmang pâr erawh;
Phuloh van sang I ni.
Theih chang se len ka nuam,
Nang nen rûn hmun khatah;
Dawn ti sei tu mai,
Kei val lung mawl tan;
Mahse pâr ang I vulna tlang mawi hi,
Beidawngin ka thlir phal lo.

2. Ka tan a hmasawn ni reng,
A inher thleng ve lo;
Ka theih tawk biahthu hlan mahila,
Dinthar rêl a rem si lo.

Kei tan a tawnmang pâr erawh;
Phuloh van sang I ni.

3. Pianpui I nih loh vang nge,
Rûnhmun I rem loh chu;
Kan inkar kawng a thui mahna,
I zûn chuan min hual reng si.

Kei tan a tawnmang pâr erawh;
Phuloh van sang I ni.

— END —
