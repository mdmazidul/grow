WRITING · POEM

The Moon

By NT Malsawma

“THE MOON”

We may behold the Moon,
Which is a blessed Boon;
Amidst the stars of the night sky,
We gazed upon her beauty passing by.

Yet we cannot have her,
For none may reach her there;
A spectacle unto the soul,
Yet burning despair unto the body whole.

A boon indeed—Yet one that leads to doom;
For what the heart may dearly seek,
The mortal hand shall never reach.

— END —
