WRITING · POEM

The Hills That Raised Me

By NT Malsawma

“THE HILLS THAT RAISED ME”

1. Wandering in the forest,
Among the harsh element;
Roaming in desperation,
Seeking for protection;
But all were in vain,
In finding rest.

2. At last! To the hills I ran,
As I always did;
It was steep with many ups,
Sweet scent of berries, but that can cut;
Way to the top was excruciating,
Teaching lessons with every fall.

3. Standing above the dark clouds below,
At the peak of the hills;
Where the winds whisper through the branches,
Laying under the shades, where shelter be sought from the sun;
A Heaven indeed from the troubles,
That cannot bother me no more.

4. The forest, the real worlds reality,
Full of temptations and problems;
A place full of life’s miseries,
The hills, a protection and a safe shelter;
A mother, a father, a family,
With love that concealed me;
The scoldings and the lectures,
That comes with the mistakes;
A lesson indeed, a bittersweet love,
They were the hills that raised me.

— END —
